Every pick goes on the record with a name and a reason, and so does every "none of these". After thirty machines, that record turned out to be the most useful thing it makes.

<ScreenCells machine="kiln" cells="rest" caption="Kiln, a struck ceramic bowl, at rest on the OP-XY's 480 by 220 screen." />

It started as a protocol for drawing on the screen the [open-xy SDK](https://github.com/etcetc000/open-xy) gives a custom machine, and turned into a server once the rules kept slipping. The [case study](../../projects/operator-design/) is the long version of how that happened. If you'd rather see it work, [the demonstration](../../projects/operator-design-demo/) walks through one machine's session a step at a time, from the five briefs written before anything is drawn to every knob at 0, 50 and 100.

Everything, the protocol, the server and the records both pages are built from, is [on GitHub](https://github.com/thoughtgrain/operator-design).
